# AFO Blueprint Walkthrough — Presenter Cheat Sheet

**Keep on a second screen or print single-sided. Bold = click action. Italic = key line.**

---

## ACT 1 — THE BRIDGE (5 min)

**Starting state:** All collapsed, all toggles off, header visible

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Point to stat cards | *"123 capabilities, 7 layers, 29 components — the most granular view of the agentic front office that exists."* |
| Pause | *"It gives us a shared language so 'agentic' means the same thing to everyone in the room."* |

C-suite exit ramp: *"This is what we'd take your team through"* → quick scroll → skip to Act 5

---

## ACT 2 — THE ARCHITECTURE (12–15 min)

### 2a. Layer Flyover (3 min)

**Scroll slowly through collapsed headers. Don't click yet.**

| Layer | One-liner |
|---|---|
| Experience | Where customers and agents touch the engine |
| AEO | The brain — decides what happens next |
| Functional Pillars | Marketing, Sales, Commerce, Service, Pricing |
| ICO | Content creation, governance, delivery |
| Commercial Brain | Customer 360, predictions, decision engines |
| Enterprise Data | Identity, integration, quality, infrastructure |
| Governance *(sidebar)* | Ethics, security, compliance — spans everything |

### 2b. Deep Dive — Pick ONE path

**Path A: Experience + AEO** ← mixed/CX audience

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 2 min | **Expand Experience layer** | 3 L1s: Conversational, Traditional, Agent-to-Agent |
| 1 min | **Click into Conversational touchpoints** | Hover on Autonomous service agent or Conversational commerce |
| 1 min | Point to Traditional channels | *"80% of interactions still flow here — agentic doesn't replace, it orchestrates"* |
| 30 sec | Point to Agent-to-agent | *"Your buyer's AI agent talks to your seller's AI agent — this is happening now"* |
| 2 min | **Collapse Experience → Expand AEO** | 3 L1s: Orchestration Core, Experience Composition, AI Workbench |
| 2 min | **Click into Orchestration Core → Human-AI balance** | Walk the 4 stages — this one always resonates |
| 1 min | Point to Experience Composition | *"Journey orchestration sits above the pillars, not inside marketing — that's the key insight"* |

**Path B: Commercial Brain** ← data/analytics audience

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 3 min | **Expand Commercial Brain** | Point out L1 components as they appear |
| 2 min | **Click into one L1 → show L2 capabilities** | *"These are the things you build, buy, or configure"* |

### 2c. Maturity Model (3 min)

**On any L2 capability already open:**

| Stage | Shorthand |
|---|---|
| 1 — Distributed | Silos, spreadsheets, tribal knowledge |
| 2 — Functional | Formalized within one function |
| 3 — Unified *(target)* | Cross-functional, single source of truth |
| 4 — Autonomous | Self-optimizing, agents deciding |

Key line: *"The goal isn't Stage 4 everywhere — it's being intentional about where you push to 3 and where you leapfrog."*

### Discussion Prompt

> *"Does this resonate? Where do you immediately see yourselves?"*

**Listen. Their answer tells you which pillars to open in Act 3.**

---

## ACT 3 — FUNCTIONAL PILLARS (10 min)

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| **Expand Functional Pillars** — 5 cards appear side by side | *"Side by side, not stacked — they run in parallel but share infrastructure above and below"* |
| **Click the pillar that matches the room** (Service for CX, Sales for revenue) | *"Let's look at [Pillar] — [X] capabilities, things like [name 2–3]"* |
| Walk one L2 through its maturity stages | *"Where would you put yourselves today?"* |
| If mixed audience: **briefly open a second pillar** | Show breadth, don't go deep |

---

## ACT 4 — DIAGNOSTICS (10–15 min)

### 4a. Platform Coverage (5 min)

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| **Toggle ON "Show platform coverage"** | *"Every capability mapped against Adobe, Salesforce, AWS, Google, Microsoft"* |
| Point to pills: green / amber / red | Strong = production-ready, Partial = needs extension, Gap = build or partner |
| **Expand Coverage Summary** | *"No platform covers everything — that's by design"* |
| *(If known stack)* **Expand Platform Fit → select their platforms** | *"Your stack covers X%, with Z genuine gaps"* |

Key line: *"This isn't a vendor bake-off — it's an architectural coverage diagnostic."*

### 4b. Assessment Mode (5 min)

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| **Toggle ON "Assessment mode"** | *"Every capability gets current state and target state ratings"* |
| **Demo on ONE capability:** click current=2, target=3, type a note | *"Facilitated conversation, not a survey"* |
| **Scroll up to show Scorecard** | *"Builds in real time — average maturity, gaps, progress by layer"* |

### 4b+. The Pre-Populated Reveal *(if applicable)*

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Assessment data already loaded — scroll through pre-filled ratings | *"We didn't come in cold — this is a hypothesis based on what we already know"* |
| Show scorecard with real numbers | *"This isn't finished — it's a starting point your team validates"* |

Power line: *"When clients see their own environment in this framework, the conversation shifts from 'is this relevant' to 'when do we start.'"*

---

## ACT 5 — THE CLOSE (5 min)

**Collapse everything. Return to top.**

> *"Shared vocabulary. Honest diagnostic. Grounded in your platforms."*

| Close style | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Assessment Sprint** — scoped to priority domains, [X] weeks | They're ready for a defined next step |
| **Broader Discovery** — assessment + journey mapping + roadmap | They want the full engagement |
| **"Where does your mind go?"** | The tool landed — let them tell you what they want |

---

## EMERGENCY HANDLING

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We're already doing this" | *"Assessment validates that — distinguishes having a tool from having a capability"* |
| "123 is overwhelming" | *"You'd assess 30–40 scoped to your priorities — the full map catches dependencies"* |
| "How vs. [Vendor X] framework?" | *"Vendor frameworks map to their products. This is platform-agnostic."* |
| "Can we get access?" | *"It's part of how we work together — your assessment becomes a deliverable"* |
| "What about [missing topic]?" | *"Living framework, v0.2. We'd incorporate industry-specific needs into your scope."* |

---

## DON'TS

- Don't open every layer — pick 2–3, let them ask
- Don't make it a vendor conversation — stay neutral
- Don't demo assessment on more than 2–3 capabilities
- Don't apologize for v0.2 — iterative rigor, not incompleteness
