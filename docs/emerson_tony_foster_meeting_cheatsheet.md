# Emerson / Tony Foster — 30-Min Qualification Call Cheat Sheet

**Keep on a second screen. Do NOT reference the $91M figure, the AI assessment, or any of Thomas's reframing.**

---

## BLOCK 1 — TONY'S NARRATIVE (0–7 min)

**Open with:** *"Thomas shared some of the work you've been doing on the digital demand conversion problem. I'd love to hear it directly from you — what brought you to this analysis and what are you trying to accomplish?"*

| Listen for | Green | Red |
|---|---|---|
| Framing | "Leadership asked me to solve this" | "My analysis / my side project" |
| Names | References executives as supporters | He is the sole voice |
| Polish | Has pitched internally, refined narrative | You're the first real audience |

**Do NOT** lead with specifics from the pre-read. Let Tony tell his story unprompted.

---

## BLOCK 2 — DATA VALIDATION (7–14 min)

| # | Question | What you're validating |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | *"The RFQ volume and leakage numbers — where do those come from? CRM data or an estimate?"* | CRM-sourced = fundable case. Estimate = hypothesis. Did finance see it? |
| Q2 | *"What's actually happening to expired RFQs? Going to competitors, through distributors, or just dying?"* | Separates real recovery opportunity from phantom. If "we don't know" — fine, supports a diagnostic. If "competitors" — ask how he knows. |
| Q3 | *"Have you quantified the silo impact — how many RFQs get routed to a rep vs. sitting in a queue?"* | Process-level detail = credible champion. High-level platitudes = thin analysis. |

---

## BLOCK 3 — CHAMPION & POLITICAL VALIDATION (14–24 min)

**This is the most important block.**

| # | Question | What you're validating |
|---|---|---|
| Q4 | *"Have you presented this to anyone in your leadership chain? What was the reaction?"* | Names + titles = green. "Trying to get airtime" = he's going external because he's stuck internally. |
| Q5 | *"If this became a real program, who would own it — your BU or corporate?"* | $15–25M doesn't live in a BU marketing budget. "BU could fund a pilot" = realistic but smaller. "Corporate" without corporate relationships = major gap. |
| Q6 | *"Where does this sit relative to the Salesforce migration and the CIO's priorities?"* | Aligned = healthy. Arguing against Salesforce = politically isolated. **Do NOT take a side on Oracle vs. Salesforce.** |
| Q7 | *"Is anyone else at Emerson — other BUs, corporate marketing, CIO's office — working on a similar problem?"* | Broader recognition = stronger enterprise case. Tony alone = $70–130M is speculative. |

---

## BLOCK 4 — COMPETITION, TIMELINE, PATH (24–29 min)

| # | Question | What you're validating |
|---|---|---|
| Q8 | *"Have you talked to any other firms, or is PwC the first conversation?"* | Competitive pursuit vs. sole-source = completely different calculus. |
| Q9 | *"What's your timeline? Planning cycle, board review, other milestone?"* | Specific date within 6 months = green. "No timeline" = drift risk. |
| Q10 | *"If we worked together, what would the ideal first step look like?"* | "Diagnostic" = healthy. "Build me a CDP" = misaligned. "Help me make the internal case" = he needs political support, not execution. Also smokes out the $150K vs. $15–25M scope gap. |

---

## CLOSE (29–30 min)

*"This is really helpful. Let me take this back to our team and come back to you with some thoughts on how we might approach this together."*

Then the key test: *"Would it be helpful if I also connected with [anyone Tony named in Block 3]?"*

- Enthusiastic yes = real champion
- Hedges = not ready, doesn't have the relationships he's implying

**Do NOT commit to scope, timeline, or team. This is a listening call.**

---

## IF TONY RAISES ORACLE vs. SALESFORCE

Redirect: *"That's an important architectural decision — we've seen it go both ways. The right answer depends on the specific use cases and data architecture. Exactly the kind of thing a diagnostic would clarify."*
